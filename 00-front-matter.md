### Preface: The Grand Unification
Physics is the study of how things move. It describes the universe using laws: Gravity, Electromagnetism, Thermodynamics.

For decades, Computer Science was treated as a branch of Mathematics. But recently, a shift occurred. As Neural Networks grew larger, they stopped behaving like logical proofs and started behaving like **physical systems**. They exhibit phase transitions, follow scaling laws, and minimize energy.

This book is born from the belief that to truly understand AI, you must stop thinking like a coder and start thinking like a physicist.
